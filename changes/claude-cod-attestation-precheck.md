**Call of Duty's security check, before the game finds it.**

### What's new

- Black Ops 7, Warzone and Modern Warfare 4 check your PC's security setup before you play online, and a PC that doesn't pass gets "Failed Attestation Status" and loses some or all online modes. When the games check finds one of them installed, that game's page now says which of the check's requirements your PC doesn't meet, and what you can do about each: Windows 10 version 22H2 or later, a TPM 2.0 that's turned on, TPM firmware the check accepts, a GPT Windows disk, UEFI start-up and Secure Boot. Its cover on the Games page says so too.
- What Heiward can't read on your PC is shown as not checked, with how to look for yourself, never as fine.
- Heiward only reads these settings: it changes nothing on your PC. Converting a disk to GPT can't be undone, so the page says to back up first, and Heiward never does it for you.

### Before you update

- Nothing: it updates itself as usual. The warning appears at the next games check, or straight away when you click **Check again** on the Games page.
