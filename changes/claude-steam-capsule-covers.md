**More Steam games show their cover.**

### What changed

- Steam has started keeping its game art under newer file names. Heiward didn't know those names yet, so games Steam had moved to them, such as Forza Horizon 6 and THE FINALS, showed a tile with only the game's name and "Steam" on the Games page. Now Heiward finds a game's cover by either name: the tall poster first, else the wide banner.
- Your own custom art in Steam still comes first, and Heiward still uses only the pictures Steam already keeps on your PC: it downloads, copies and resizes nothing.
- Covers are found during the games check. After the update, the missing covers appear at the next check, or straight away when you click **Check again** on the Games page.

### Before you update

- Nothing: it updates itself as usual.
