**The top of the window is one row, with a Home button beside Up, and the home page's foot moved to Settings.**

### What changed

- Back, Forward, Up, the new **Home** button and the path now share one row with the scan's status, where the AI runs, Pause, About, Settings, Theme and **Scan now**. Before, they took two rows.
- **Home** (a house, beside Up) goes to This PC from anywhere, as Heiward's shield and the "Home" label did. The shield and the label are gone.
- The path is shorter: it takes the room left on the row, up to about a third of a wide window. A path too long for it keeps This PC and the folders nearest the one you're in, with **…** standing for the folders in between. Click **…** to go to any of them. Point at the path to see all of it.
- In a narrower window the path gives way first. Then, one at a time: the way back to the manor keeps just its icon, the AI badge shrinks to its dot and a word ("GPU"), and the scan's status steps aside (point at **Scan now** to see it). In a very narrow window the path takes a row of its own. Nothing overlaps, and the page never scrolls sideways.
- What the bottom of the home page said (what's scanned, file types left out and, in developer mode, the settings file) is now only in **Settings**, under **Everything else**, which also says that the folders left out show greyed out.

### Before you update
