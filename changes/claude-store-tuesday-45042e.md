**Heiward from the Microsoft Store can now update on Tuesdays too.**

### What changed

- The Microsoft Store gets Heiward's newest release twice a week now: on Tuesday afternoons (2 pm New York time) as well as on Friday mornings (10 am). If you installed Heiward from the Store, a new version can reach you on either day, once the Store has checked it.
- The Store still checks one version at a time. A version sent on Tuesday that's still being checked on Friday goes first, and the newer one follows on the next Tuesday or Friday.

### Before you update

- Nothing: it updates itself as usual.
