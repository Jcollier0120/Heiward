**A new install asks about its first scan once you're on the home page, not before.**

### What changed

- When the setup finishes, its last page no longer has the yellow bar with **Scan now** and **Scan by itself** across the top. That page already says what happens next: open Heiward, and its tour ends by asking whether to scan now or let it scan by itself.
- While the tour shows, the bar waits too, since the tour's last step asks the same thing.
- If you skip the tour, close it with Esc, or choose **Later**, the bar is there on the home page, so the first scan still has something asking about it. It also comes back when you reopen Heiward before its first scan has run.
- Taking the tour again from About or Settings hides the bar only while the tour shows.
- The tour's **Your choices** step no longer has the **Game mode** and **Developer mode** switches when the setup has just asked about them. That's always the case for Heiward from the Microsoft Store. Settings still has both.
- Just after the setup, the home page could say "Scheduled scans aren't running" for up to half an hour, though they were set up. Heiward now checks again as soon as the setup ends.

### Before you update

- Nothing: it updates itself as usual.
