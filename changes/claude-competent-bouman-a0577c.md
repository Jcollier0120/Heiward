**Another of your PCs with an NPU can now scan this PC's photos and videos for it, over Castellan.**

### What's new

- With a Castellan license for more than one PC, another of your PCs can find the copies among this PC's photos and videos for it, on its own NPU. Under Settings, **Your other PCs** offers **Have <PC> scan this PC** when another PC on your license runs the same version of Heiward and its NPU is free.
- This PC only lists its photos and videos and hands over the parts the other PC asks for: at very low disk priority, in Windows' efficiency mode, about 20 MB a second, and never while a game is played here (a game that starts midway pauses it). The parts go over Castellan's encrypted link between your PCs, and the other PC keeps none of them: each file's bytes are let go as soon as they're read.
- Only what's needed crosses: each photo once, and a video's header and the frames that are compared. A file is read whole only to confirm two plain copies. Later scans read only the files that are new or changed; with nothing new, a scan takes seconds.
- The card shows the scan's progress here as it goes, and it can be stopped.
- What the other PC finds shows here with this PC's own sets, marked **Found by <PC>**. Nothing is removed without you: you clean up with the usual buttons, with the usual checks before anything goes to the Recycle Bin, and automatic cleanup leaves these sets alone. **Let go of them** clears them.
- The PC doing the scan uses at most 8 of its processor's cores, in the background, and the AI on its NPU, and pauses while a game runs there.
- Its NPU's more urgent work goes first: while that PC's NPU captions a call, or does work someone is waiting on, the scan waits, and then goes on where it was. The card here says so meanwhile.

### What changed

- Reading the words in photos on the NPU now takes turns with the other programs that use the NPU, as finding look-alikes already did.

### Before you update

- Both PCs need this version of Heiward, and Castellan with **Other PCs** turned on under The estate. If the other PC runs another version, the card says to update Heiward there.
- Update Castellan on both PCs too: its newest version moves files between your PCs faster on a Snapdragon PC.
