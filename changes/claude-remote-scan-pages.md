**Another of your PCs can now scan this one however many photos and videos it has.**

### What changed

- A PC with a very big library, hundreds of thousands of photos and videos, can now be scanned by another of your PCs. Its list of files goes over in pages of a few megabytes each. Before, a list that big stopped the scan at "Asking for its list of files", and nothing was scanned.
- The PC being scanned keeps that list on its disk while the other PC scans it, not in memory: for 700,000 files it holds about 12 MB, where it held more than a gigabyte. The memory it used to make the list goes back to Windows straight after.
- If the list changes while it goes over, the scan asks for it again from the start, so it always works from one list.
- When a scan can't go on, the card says what happened and what to do, in plain words. When the two PCs run different versions of Heiward, it says which one to update.

### Before you update

- Both PCs need this version of Heiward for one to scan the other; Castellan updates it on both. The first scan after the update asks for the other PC's whole list of files once; later scans read only what's new, as before.
