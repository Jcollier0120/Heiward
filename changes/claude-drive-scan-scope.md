**Scan this drive now reads just that drive.**

### What changed

- On a drive Heiward scans by itself, **Scan this drive now** now reads only that drive. Before, it scanned every drive Heiward scans by itself. The other drives stay as their last scans found them, so a disk that sleeps stays asleep, and their duplicates stay listed.
- Only that drive's "Last scanned" changes. The scheduled scans keep their times and still read every drive, and nothing is skipped: **Scan now instead of at 6:00 PM** is still the button that stands in for the next scheduled scan.
- If you chose at setup that Heiward scans only when you press Scan now, **Scan this drive now** on a drive's page reads only that drive too. **Scan now** still reads them all.
- On a drive Heiward scans only when you ask, **Scan this drive now** still reads that drive along with the drives Heiward scans by itself.
- After updating, if your first scan reads just one drive or one folder, the other drives now keep their "Last scanned" time. Before, they could show "Last scanned: never" until the next full scan.

### Before you update

- Nothing: it updates itself as usual.
